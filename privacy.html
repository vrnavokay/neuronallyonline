Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026

Official Neuronally only

Neuronally is free of charge, and we collect nothing. We never ask for payment, passwords, Roblox credentials, API keys, two-factor codes or account details. Copycat websites and Discord servers may pretend to be us. Do not download, pay or sign in anywhere that is not linked from this site.

1. The short version

Neuronally does not collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no profiles, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no telemetry and no advertising. The app is free to use, and this website is a static set of pages.

We do not want your personal data, we do not ask for it, and we do not sell or share it.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • Account credentials, passwords or two-factor codes.
  • Roblox usernames, passwords, cookies or session tokens.
  • API keys, payment details or billing information.
  • Analytics, page views, click events, heatmaps or session recordings.
  • Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints or location data.
  • Crash reports, diagnostics or usage telemetry from the app.
  • Scripts, tabs, settings, logs or files from your workspace.

There is no account to create and no profile to fill in. If a page or person asks for any of the above while claiming to be Neuronally, it is not us.

3. Free of charge

Neuronally is free of charge. There is no subscription, no card, no trial period, no paid tier, no license key and no account upgrade. We do not ask for payment anywhere, and we will not charge for access.

If another website offers a paid version of Neuronally, asks for a card or charges for access, it is not official. Close it and return to this site.

4. What stays on your device

Neuronally runs locally. Scripts, tabs, settings, backups and console logs are stored on your own machine. We do not receive copies, and there is no cloud sync, remote backup or account-linked storage.

Removing the app or deleting its files removes that local data. Because we never had it, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

5. Cookies and analytics

We do not set cookies, and we do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks, fingerprinting or session recording. There is no cookie banner because there are no optional cookies to consent to.

The site loads its display font from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google, which may receive standard request information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive, store or use that information. If you prefer to avoid it, your browser or an extension can block the request and the site will fall back to a system font.

6. Third-party links

The site links to Discord, where the community lives, and may link to other outside services. Those services have their own privacy policies and may collect data when you open them. We do not control them, and we do not receive anything they collect.

7. Impersonation websites

Fake sites, fake downloads and fake Discord servers are a real risk for any free tool. Treat every other address, mirror, "official mirror", paid download page or credential form as impersonation unless this site links to it.

Neuronally will never ask you to:

  • Pay for access, a license, a key or a "pro" version.
  • Enter your Roblox password, cookie, session token or two-factor code.
  • Send API keys, wallet details or personal documents.
  • Disable your antivirus or run an unknown "fix" before using the app.

If a page asks for any of these, stop. Do not download or sign in. Report it in the community Discord so other people can avoid it too.

8. Children

Neuronally is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since we collect nothing at all, there is no child data for us to hold.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws give people rights to access, correct, export or delete their personal data. We cannot act on those requests in the usual way because we do not hold personal data about you. If you believe we have something, contact us and we will check. There are no accounts to close and no data to port.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will change first. We do not plan to start collecting data. Any future change would be described here in plain language before it took effect.

11. Contact

Questions, concerns or impersonation reports can go to the community. Reach us in the Neuronally Discord. We cannot recover accounts or reset passwords because Neuronally does not have accounts.
